Observations:
1. The transformation expands colored squares vertically and horizontally.
2. The expansion prioritizes the direction with more available space.
3. If space is equal in both directions, it expands upwards.
4. The expansion creates T-shapes, inverted T-shapes, or rectangles.
5. The expansion should not overwrite existing colored squares.

3. The vertical expansion (stem of the T) is extending too far in some cases.
