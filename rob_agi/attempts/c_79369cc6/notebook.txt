Challenge 79369cc6:

Problem Description:
- Transform an input grid by adding a new yellow-magenta formation.
- The formation should be placed in the quadrant that results in the most balanced distribution of yellow (4) and magenta (6) cells across the entire grid.
- The formation can be either 3x3 or 2x2, placed in the corner of the chosen quadrant closest to the grid center.
- Avoid overwriting existing yellow or magenta cells.
- Only one transformation is applied per grid.

What we know:
1. The grid is divided into four quadrants.
2. The formation consists of yellow (4) and magenta (6) cells in a specific pattern.
3. The balance is determined by the total count of yellow and magenta cells across the entire grid.

What we've learned from the latest test:
1. The current implementation is placing the formation in the wrong quadrant (top-left instead of top-right).
2. The formation pattern is incorrect. It should be:
   - For 3x3: Yellow (4) in the corner closest to center and its two adjacent cells, magenta (6) in the rest.
   - For 2x2: Yellow (4) in the corners, magenta (6) in the other two cells.

What needs to be fixed:
1. The quadrant selection logic needs to be revised to choose the correct quadrant.
2. The formation pattern needs to be corrected to match the expected output.
3. The balance calculation should consider the entire grid, not just the quadrants.

Next steps:
1. Revise the quadrant selection logic to properly evaluate the balance across the entire grid.
2. Correct the formation pattern in the add_formation function.
3. Ensure that the formation is placed in the corner closest to the grid center in the chosen quadrant.
4. Double-check that we're not overwriting existing yellow or magenta cells.
