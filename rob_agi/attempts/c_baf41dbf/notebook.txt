Challenge baf41dbf:

Problem Description:
- Transform a 2D grid by expanding a green (3) shape while respecting boundaries set by magenta (6) dots and grid edges.
- The internal structure of the green shape should be preserved and scaled appropriately.
- The grid dimensions remain the same after transformation.

What we know:
1. The green shape expands horizontally and vertically until it reaches a magenta dot or the grid edge.
2. The internal structure (black cells within the green shape) must be maintained and scaled.
3. Magenta dots remain in their original positions and should not be overwritten.

What we've learned from the latest test:
1. Our current implementation is expanding the shape correctly horizontally.
2. The vertical expansion is incorrect - it's filling the entire height of the grid instead of respecting the top and bottom boundaries.
3. The internal structure is being preserved but not scaled correctly.

What needs to be fixed:
1. Vertical expansion calculation needs to be adjusted to respect top and bottom boundaries.
2. Scaling of the internal structure needs to be more accurate.
3. The top-left corner of the expanded shape should remain at (1,1) instead of (0,0).

Next steps:
1. Modify the vertical expansion calculation in the find_boundary function.
2. Adjust the mapping of original cells to new positions to ensure correct scaling of internal structure.
3. Ensure the expanded shape starts at the correct position (1,1) instead of (0,0).

Things that don't work:
- Simply expanding the shape to fill the entire available space without proper scaling.
- Ignoring the original position of the green shape when expanding.
