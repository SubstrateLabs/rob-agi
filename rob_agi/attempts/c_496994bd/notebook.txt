Challenge 496994bd:

Pattern observed:
- Non-zero rows at the top of the input grid are mirrored to the bottom.
- The order of the mirrored rows is important and should be preserved.

What we know (with certainty):
1. The function should identify consecutive non-zero rows from the top of the input grid.
2. These rows should be mirrored to the bottom of the grid in the same order they appear at the top.
3. The middle section (zero rows) remains unchanged.

What we've learned:
1. Simply reversing the order of rows when mirroring doesn't work.
2. Keeping the same order when mirroring from top to bottom also doesn't work.
3. The correct approach is to mirror the rows from the bottom up, reversing their order in the process.

Current implementation:
- The mirroring logic now starts from the bottom of the grid and works upwards.
- The order of the non-zero rows is reversed during the mirroring process.
- This approach correctly preserves the order of the rows as they appear at the top of the grid.
