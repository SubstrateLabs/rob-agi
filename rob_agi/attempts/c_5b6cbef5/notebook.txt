2. The expansion seems to preserve the overall structure and density of the input pattern.
3. There's a tendency to fill larger areas with the dominant color from the input.

What doesn't work:
1. Simple 4x4 replication of the input pattern.
2. Expanding each cell of the input into a 4x4 block in the output.

Possible approach:
1. Analyze the input grid for key features:
   - Dominant color
   - Edge patterns
   - Full rows/columns
   - Symmetry

3. Apply the input pattern to specific areas of the output grid:
   - Top-left, top-right, bottom-left, and bottom-right 4x4 corners
   - Center 8x8 area
4. Preserve edge patterns by extending them across the entire edge of the output.
5. Handle full rows/columns by extending them across the entire output.
6. Apply symmetry if detected in the input.
7. Fill in remaining areas based on the density and distribution of colors in the input.
