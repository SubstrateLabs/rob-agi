Challenge 08573cc6: Transform input grid into a specific pattern

What we know:
1. The input grid is 11x11 or 12x12.
2. There are two colored squares in the top-left corner: main_color at (0,0) and side_color at (0,1).
3. There's a single colored square (usually blue) somewhere in the grid.

Pattern characteristics:
1. The output forms a rectangular shape with nested frames.
2. The outer frame uses main_color for top, bottom, and left edges; side_color for the right edge.
3. The inner frame uses side_color for top, left, and right edges; main_color for the bottom edge.
4. The single colored square is positioned inside the inner frame, slightly off-center.
5. There are main_color squares adjacent to the single colored square.
6. The remaining space inside the inner frame is filled with main_color, leaving a 1-cell gap around the single colored square and its adjacent main_color squares.

What we've learned from the latest test failure:
1. Our current implementation is creating a larger pattern than expected.
2. The outer frame should start at row 4, not row 2 as in our current implementation.
3. We're filling too much of the inner area with the main_color.
4. The positioning of the single colored square and its adjacent main_color squares is incorrect.

What to try next:
1. Adjust the starting position of the outer frame to row 4.
2. Reduce the size of the inner filled area.
3. Correct the positioning of the single colored square and its adjacent main_color squares.
4. Ensure that there's a 1-cell gap of empty (black) space around the single colored square and its adjacent main_color squares.

Things that don't work:
1. Starting the outer frame at row 2.
2. Filling the entire inner frame with the main_color.

Unknowns:
