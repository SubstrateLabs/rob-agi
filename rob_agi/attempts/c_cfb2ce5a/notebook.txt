Challenge cfb2ce5a: Grid Expansion with Pattern Preservation

What we know:
1. The challenge involves expanding a 10x10 grid while preserving original patterns and maintaining a black border.
2. Each color needs to expand from its original position(s) while maintaining relative positioning.
3. The expansion should fill the entire grid except for the border.

What we've learned:
1. Our current implementation is not correctly preserving the original patterns for all colors.
2. The expansion of some colors (like blue 1 and sky blue 8) is too aggressive, overtaking areas that should be occupied by other colors.
3. The relative positioning of colors in the expanded grid is not matching the expected output.

What doesn't work:
1. Simple expansion based on initial frequencies without considering spatial relationships.
2. Treating all colors equally during expansion without considering their initial patterns.

What we need to improve:
1. Implement a more sophisticated pattern recognition and preservation mechanism.
2. Develop a method to maintain the relative positioning of colors during expansion.
3. Create a balance between expanding isolated colors and preserving larger initial patterns.

Next steps:
1. Analyze the initial grid to identify distinct patterns for each color (e.g., clusters, lines, isolated points).
2. Implement expansion rules that respect these identified patterns.
3. Develop a method to divide the grid into regions based on initial color positions and expand within these regions.
4. Implement a priority system for expansion based on initial color frequencies and patterns.
5. Add a final pass to ensure connectivity between isolated instances of the same color.

Unknowns:
1. The exact rules for determining how far each color should expand.
2. How to handle conflicts when expanding patterns intersect.
