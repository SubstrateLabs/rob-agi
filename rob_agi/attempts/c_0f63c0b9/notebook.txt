Challenge 0f63c0b9: Transform a 15x15 grid with scattered colored squares into a grid with color frames.

Key observations:
1. Colors are processed from top to bottom based on their first appearance.
2. Each color creates a frame-like structure.
3. There should be at least one black row between color sections.
4. Each color section has a minimum height of 3 rows.
5. Space is distributed evenly among colors, with preference given to top and bottom colors.

Frame structure:
- Top color: Two full rows at the top, vertical lines extending down.
- Middle colors: One full row in the middle, vertical lines extending to boundaries.
- Bottom color: Two full rows at the bottom, vertical lines extending up.

- The space distribution algorithm needs refinement.
- The handling of black separator rows is not perfect.

- Using a fixed +2 for the black separator row.

3. Implement a more flexible approach to adding black separator rows.
