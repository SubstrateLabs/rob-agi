Challenge da2b0fe3: Grid Transformation

Objective: Add a green line (color 3) to the input grid, intersecting with the existing shape.

Key Observations:
1. The green line should span the entire grid (either horizontally or vertically).
2. The line's position is determined by the center and symmetry of the existing shape.

Current Implementation:
- Finds the bounding box of non-zero elements.
- Calculates the center of the shape.
- Determines symmetry by comparing left/right and top/bottom halves.
- Chooses vertical or horizontal line based on symmetry scores.

Issues Identified:
- The implementation correctly handles vertical line placement (Example 2).
- It fails for horizontal line placement (Example 1).

Possible Improvements:
1. Refine symmetry calculation:
   - Consider the shape's overall structure, not just element-wise comparison.
2. Adjust center calculation:
   - May need to weight the center based on the shape's distribution.
3. Implement a tie-breaker for symmetry:
   - When vertical and horizontal symmetry are similar, prefer horizontal line.

Next Steps:
1. Revise symmetry calculation to better capture the shape's overall structure.
2. Implement a more robust tie-breaking mechanism for symmetry.
3. Test with various input shapes to ensure consistent behavior.

Remember: The green line must always span the entire grid, regardless of the shape's size.
