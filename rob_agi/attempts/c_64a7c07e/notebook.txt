Challenge 64a7c07e: Grid Transformation

Objective:
- Transform input grid by horizontally shifting shapes while maintaining vertical positions.
- Preserve the relative order of shapes from left to right.
- Center the overall arrangement of shapes within the grid.

Key Observations:
1. The algorithm successfully centers larger shapes (example_0 and example_2 pass).
2. Smaller grids with shapes close to the edge (example_1) are not correctly centered.

Current Implementation:
- Uses DFS to identify connected shapes.
- Calculates ideal gaps between shapes based on available space.
- Attempts to center the entire arrangement.

Issue Identified:
- The centering calculation doesn't work correctly for smaller grids or when shapes are close to the grid edges.

Next Steps:
1. Refine the centering calculation to work for all grid sizes.
2. Ensure that shapes are moved to the right even in small grids.
3. Consider edge cases where shapes are already at the rightmost position.

Things Tried That Didn't Work:
- Simple distribution of shapes across available space without considering grid size.

Certainties:
- The algorithm correctly identifies and preserves shape structures.
- The relative order of shapes is maintained.

Uncertainties:
- The exact formula for calculating the optimal starting position for different grid sizes.

To Investigate:
