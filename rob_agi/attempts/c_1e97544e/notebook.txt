Challenge 1e97544e: Grid Transformation

Key Observations:
1. The challenge involves transforming a grid by filling in black (0) squares with appropriate colors to maintain a diagonal pattern.
2. The color sequence is determined by the first row, skipping initial repetitions and black squares.
3. The pattern shifts one position to the right in each subsequent row, creating a diagonal effect.
4. Some rows have repeated colors at the beginning, which should be preserved.

What Works:
- Identifying the main color sequence from the first row.
- Preserving initial repeated colors at the beginning of each row.
- Maintaining original non-zero colors.

What Doesn't Work:
- The current implementation fails to correctly fill in all black squares, especially in complex patterns.
- The diagonal shift is not consistently maintained across all rows.

Current Issues:
1. The algorithm doesn't correctly handle the transition between preserved initial repetitions and the main pattern.
2. The diagonal shift calculation needs refinement, especially for rows with initial repetitions.
3. The pattern continuity across rows is not fully achieved, particularly in areas with multiple consecutive black squares.

Next Steps:
