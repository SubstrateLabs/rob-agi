Challenge 9772c176: Adding Yellow Shadows to Sky Blue Shapes

Key Observations:
1. The challenge involves adding yellow (4) shadows to sky blue (8) shapes on a black (0) background.
2. Shadows should not directly touch the sky blue shapes; there must be a one-pixel black buffer.
3. Shadows are more pronounced on the bottom and right sides of shapes.
4. There should be a visual connection (bridge) between separate sky blue shapes using yellow pixels.
5. Some scattered yellow pixels appear in the black space, especially towards the bottom-right.

Current Implementation Issues:
1. The shadow is too extensive, covering almost the entire grid with yellow pixels.
2. The one-pixel buffer between blue and yellow is not maintained.
3. The directional bias (bottom-right emphasis) is not correctly implemented.
4. The shadow doesn't follow the contours of the sky blue shapes closely enough.

Next Steps:
1. Adjust the shadow intensity calculation to limit the spread of yellow pixels.
2. Implement a strict check to ensure no yellow pixel touches a sky blue pixel.
3. Refine the directional bias to emphasize bottom and right sides more accurately.
4. Improve the algorithm for creating bridges between shapes.
5. Add a more controlled method for scattered yellow pixels in black space.

Failed Approaches:
1. Using a simple distance-based shadow map without considering shape contours.
2. Applying a uniform shadow around the entire shape without directional bias.

Open Questions:
1. What's the ideal maximum distance for shadow spread from sky blue shapes?
2. How to balance between connected shadows and scattered pixels?
3. What's the best way to create natural-looking bridges between shapes?
