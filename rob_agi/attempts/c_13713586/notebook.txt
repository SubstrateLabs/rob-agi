Challenge 13713586: Expand colored regions in a grid

Key observations:
1. Expansion occurs column-wise, from left to right.
2. Within each column, vertical expansion happens first, then horizontal.
3. Colors should not overwrite each other during expansion.
4. Gray (5) borders remain unchanged and limit expansion.

Algorithm outline:
1. Process columns from left to right.
2. For each column:
   a. Expand colors vertically within the column.
   b. Expand colors horizontally to the right, limited by vertical expansions and previous column expansions.
3. Preserve original non-black colors and gray borders.

Current issues:
1. Horizontal expansion is not respecting the order of colors from top to bottom.
2. Vertical expansion is occurring in all columns simultaneously, rather than column by column.
3. Colors are overwriting each other, especially in later columns.

Next steps:
1. Modify the algorithm to process one column at a time, completing both vertical and horizontal expansions before moving to the next column.
2. Implement a mechanism to prevent colors from overwriting each other during horizontal expansion.
3. Ensure that the order of colors within a column is preserved during expansion.

Things tried that didn't work:
1. Expanding all colors vertically first, then horizontally - this led to incorrect overwriting of colors.

Unknowns:
1. The exact rules for resolving conflicts when multiple colors could expand into the same space.
2. Whether there are any special cases or exceptions to the general expansion rules.
